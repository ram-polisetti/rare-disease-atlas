Subway view (optional)

The mechanism connections drawn as a transit map. Each line is a biological pathway; stations are diseases; transfer stations are diseases on more than one line, where mechanisms overlap and research may be shared.

What this map does NOT mean
  • Lines are not treatment routes — a line only groups diseases that share a recorded biological mechanism.
  • Intersections do not mean the diseases are biologically close or that a treatment for one will work for another.
  • Distance on this map measures nothing about biology — it is only layout spacing.
