# Demo video script (60 seconds max)

Show the project in action with clear narration. Record against the live demo at https://ram-polisetti.github.io/rare-disease-atlas/. Zoom to about 125%. Pause narration during clicks.

**0:00 to 0:12, Guide search** [Guide tab. Click "Skip — just let me search". Type "sanfilippo". Show the side-by-side matches.]
"I type 'sanfilippo.' The atlas shows what it understood: five matching conditions, side by side, with how the data tells them apart."

**0:12 to 0:24, Your Disease** [Pick Sanfilippo A (MPS IIIA).]
"Sanfilippo A: its gene, symptoms, active trials, patient groups, and treatment status, including Fayuvi, the gene therapy approved September 17th, 2026."

**0:24 to 0:36, Explore** [Explore tab. Click an edge, show the evidence panel.]
"The Explore tab is the knowledge graph behind it. Click any link and the evidence panel shows the source, the exact quote, and a plain confidence label."

**0:36 to 0:48, Endpoints** [Endpoints tab, sharing matrix.]
"Endpoints shows which diseases could share trial measurements in future trials, with the honest caveat stated on the page: sharing a measurement is not sharing biology."

**0:48 to 1:00, The 10x Case and close** [The 10x Case tab, the two bars.]
"The 10x Case models one shared study design cutting a trial timeline from about 5.5 years to 2.2. Modeled, not measured. Plain language, print-ready reports, every claim traceable to its source."
